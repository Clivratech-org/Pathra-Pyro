Compliance

Legal & Regulatory Information

Transparency on how we operate within Indian fireworks trade regulations.

01

Supreme Court Notice

In line with Hon'ble Supreme Court of India orders, we do not process any online payment for firecracker sales. All listed prices are indicative.

02

Online Sales Restriction

Firecrackers cannot legally be sold via e-commerce checkout. This website functions as a product catalogue and enquiry system only.

03

Order by Enquiry Only

Items added to cart generate an enquiry that is confirmed manually by our licensed dealer over WhatsApp or phone call.

04

Licensed Dealer Information

Sri Pathra Pyro World operates under Explosives License No. TN/VIR/EXP/0857/2025, issued by the Petroleum & Explosives Safety Organisation (PESO).

05

GST Registration

GSTIN: 33AFRFS8857B1ZJ — registered under the Tamil Nadu Goods and Services Tax Act.

06

Transport Policy

All shipments move exclusively through PESO-approved explosive-goods transport carriers with valid road permits.

⚠️

Important Notice to Customers

Cracker bursting timings, categories permitted, and green-cracker mandates may vary by state/local authority notification. Please check your local municipal guidelines before use. Prices and stock are subject to change without prior notice.
